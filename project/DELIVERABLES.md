# Required Deliverables

1. **D1 Discovery & Requirements** — stakeholder map, problem statement, user stories, requirements, acceptance criteria. `docs/01-discovery/`
2. **D2 Data Governance & Source Assessment** — source register, glossary, critical data elements, ownership, lineage, DQ rules. `docs/02-data-governance/`
3. **D3 Ingestion & Transformation** — reproducible acquisition and transformation. `src/`, `sql/`, `docs/03-data-model/`
4. **D4 Data Quality & Analytical Model** — profiling, validation, exceptions, grain/model. `docs/04-data-quality/`
5. **D5 KPI Catalogue** — definition, purpose, formula, grain, source, owner, exclusions, limitations. `docs/05-kpi-catalogue/`
6. **D6 Core Analytical Assessment** — FTE/headcount capacity, workforce mix, turnover, organisational variation, occupational pressure and sustainability indicators. `analysis/`, `docs/06-analysis/`
7. **D7 Decision/Prioritisation Framework** — transparent evidence-to-decision logic, assumptions and limitations. `docs/07-decision-framework/`
8. **D8 BI / Decision-Support Product** — governed measures, usable navigation and decision-oriented views. `dashboard/`
9. **D9 Assurance & UAT** — test scenarios, reconciliations, UAT evidence, issues and acceptance. `docs/08-assurance-uat/`
10. **D10 Technical & Operational Handover** — setup, architecture, refresh/run, controls, failure points and maintenance. `docs/09-technical-handover/`
11. **D11 Executive Briefing & Presentation** — concise decision, evidence, recommendations, risks and next actions. `deliverables/`
12. **D12 Contribution Record** — actual role, artefacts and linked GitHub evidence. `CONTRIBUTIONS.md`
